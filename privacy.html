Privacy Policy

BreathPal · Effective date: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the BreathPal iOS app ("BreathPal", "the app") handles your information. BreathPal is made by Skyler Williams ("we", "us"). In short: your training data stays on your device, you don't need an account, and we don't track you.

1. Data stored on your device only

BreathPal stores the following locally on your iPhone, and nowhere else:

  • The name you enter, your experience level, and your personal best times
  • Your training plan, session history, streaks, badges, and companion progress
  • Your app settings (audio, haptics, voice, reminders) and any custom timers or coach presets

This data is never sent to us. We have no servers that receive it, and we cannot see it. There is no account and no sign-in.

2. Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see or store your payment details. To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives:

  • A random, anonymous app user ID generated by the app (not your name, email, or Apple ID)
  • Purchase information: which subscription you bought, transaction and receipt data, trial and renewal status, price and currency
  • Basic technical data needed to process requests, such as device type, OS and app version, country/region, and IP address

RevenueCat uses this only to provide subscription services. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. No ads, no tracking, no analytics

BreathPal contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking across apps or websites. We do not sell or share your personal information.

4. Notifications

Training reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is involved and no notification token is collected.

5. Keeping, exporting, and deleting your data

  • Export: Settings → Export Data shares a copy of your data.
  • Delete: Settings → Reset All Data erases everything stored by the app. Deleting the app also removes it.
  • Purchase records: to ask about data held by RevenueCat for your anonymous ID, email us at the address below.

6. Children

BreathPal is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have rights to access or delete personal information. Because your training data never leaves your device, you control it directly in the app. For anything else, contact us.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
