2. Health and safety disclaimer

  • Dry training only. Use BreathPal only on land, seated or lying down in a safe place. Never practise breath-holds in or near water, in a bath or pool, while driving, or while operating anything.
  • Never train breath-holds in water alone. In-water breath-holding without trained supervision can cause shallow-water blackout and drowning.
  • Never hyperventilate before a breath hold.
  • Stop immediately if you feel dizzy, lightheaded, tingling, confused, or unwell. Never push through warning signs.
  • Not medical advice. BreathPal provides general training information for educational purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.
  • Consult a doctor before starting, especially if you have heart, lung, blood-pressure, or seizure conditions, are pregnant, or have any other medical concern.
